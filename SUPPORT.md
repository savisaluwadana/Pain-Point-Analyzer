# Support and feedback

Open Source Discovery Lab is an **alpha** and comes with **community best-effort** support, not a managed service or a guaranteed SLA.

Start with the [five-minute demo](docs/DEMO.md) and [connector reference](docs/SOURCES.md). Use [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/savisaluwadana/Pain-Point-Analyzer/issues) for reproducible bugs, questions about setup, and improvement proposals; the issue templates help us triage.

When asking for help, share your OS, Docker/Compose version, the step attempted, the relevant HTTP status and **redacted** logs. Do not share tokens, session cookies, passwords, private post text or personal data.

Do not file security disclosures publicly: follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Be patient with volunteer response times.
