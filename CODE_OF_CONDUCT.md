# Community Code of Conduct

We want Open Source Discovery Lab to be a welcoming space for contributors of different backgrounds, experience levels
and perspectives.

## Expected behavior

- Communicate respectfully, especially when reviewing technical disagreements.
- Offer specific, constructive feedback and give credit to other contributors.
- Respect privacy, informed consent and the rules of the communities whose data is discussed.
- Help newer contributors and make room for different approaches.
- Follow maintainers' reasonable moderation requests.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, threats or personal attacks.
- Unwanted sexual attention, stalking or revealing someone else's private information.
- Abusive or disruptive comments, deliberate misrepresentation of others, or targeted harassment.
- Publishing private tokens, identifiable user data, or security exploits to shame or intimidate others.

## Scope and enforcement

This applies to GitHub issues, pull requests, reviews and any other project-designated community space. Maintainers may
edit or remove comments, close discussions or restrict participation where needed.

For sensitive behavior reports, contact the repository maintainer **privately** using a contact channel advertised on
their GitHub profile. If none is available, open a neutral issue asking for a private reporting channel **without
including personal or sensitive allegations in public**. Maintainers will handle reports confidentially to the extent
practical, without promising a fixed response time.

Good-faith technical disagreement, critical feedback and discussion of alternative design choices are welcome.
